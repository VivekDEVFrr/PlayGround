FAQ

Common questions

How do I join the server?

Join the Discord, get through the existing whitelist flow, then connect to tarzan.supernovaa.in:25509.

Do I need to re-apply?

No. The whitelist system is unchanged and existing approvals stay valid.

Bedrock or Java?

Both. Java uses port 25509, Bedrock uses 19132 on the same host.

Where do I appeal a ban?

Open a General support ticket in Discord and tag a staff member.
