House rules

Be the neighbour you'd want.

Five rules, no fine print. Break one and staff will have a very boring conversation with you.

01

Build, don't bulldoze

No griefing, no stealing, no "it was unclaimed". Leave a sign if you're expanding near someone.

02

Play fair

No x-ray, auto-clickers, duping or alt-account farming. Optifine, shaders and minimaps are fine.

03

Keep chat human

Banter yes, harassment no. Slurs, doxxing and spam get you removed without a debate.

04

Trade honestly

Agree prices in chat. Scamming a new player out of their first diamonds is embarrassing.

05

One person, one account

Alts for storage are fine with staff approval. Alts to dodge a ban are not.
